## OUTREACH STRATEGY OVERVIEW

### Timing & Priority

**Week 1 (Immediate):** Printful, Printify, Beechfield  
**Week 2:** Result Headwear, Flexfit distributor  
**Week 3:** Atlantis, Fifth Column, others  

### Key Principles

1. **Be specific:** Mention design type, quantities, timeline
2. **Be professional:** Clear writing, polite, concise
3. **Provide context:** Who is MEGA Caps? Why caps?
4. **Ask clear questions:** Quote, samples, lead time, services
5. **Follow up:** 48-72 hours if no response, then once weekly
6. **Personalize:** Use correct contact name, reference company details

### Success Metrics

- Response rate: 70%+ (within 48–72 hours)
- Quote precision: Specific pricing, no vague estimates
- Sample turnaround: <7 days quoted, <14 days delivered
- Professional tone: Clear communication, easy follow-up

## TEMPLATE 1: POD PLATFORM OUTREACH (Phase 1 Testing)

**To:** Printful / Printify support (or sales team)  
**Subject:** Cap Design Testing & Sample Order : potential High-Volume Customer

---

### Email Body

```
Hi [Team/Name],

I'm launching MEGA Caps, a premium UK cap brand, and I'm looking to validate 
designs before scaling to full production.

I'm interested in using [Printful/Printify] to test 3–5 design variations with

- Product: Caps (6-panel structure, adjustable strap or snapback)

with POD to validate market fit.

in 2026 if demand warrants.

- A detailed pricing quote (with example 25-unit order)?
- Link to design templates or upload process?
- A sample order form or process?

Thanks,
[Your Name]
MEGA Caps (megacaps.co.uk)
[Phone]
[Email]
```

## TONE GUIDE

### Good Tone: Professional, Specific, Action-Oriented
✅ "I'm launching MEGA Caps and looking to order 100 embroidered units. Here are my specs. Can you provide a quote and sample?"

### Avoid: Vague, Desperate, Unprofessional
❌ "Hey, I'm making caps lol, can you make some for me? How much?"
❌ "I need caps ASAP!!!!!!"

### Be Specific
✅ "100 units, embroidered front, 3 colours, 2-week lead time acceptable"  
❌ "Some caps, needs to be good quality, asap"

### Show Professionalism
✅ Include company website, logo, elevator pitch  
❌ Use personal Gmail, no business info  

### Be Realistic About Timeline
✅ "2–4 weeks acceptable"  
❌ "Need it tomorrow"

## RESPONSE HANDLING

### If Response is Slow (>3 Days)
- Send follow-up (friendly, not aggressive)
- Consider this a signal about supplier responsiveness
- Don't wait indefinitely; explore alternatives

### If Quote is Vague
Ask for clarification:
- "Does this include embroidery?"
- "What's included in the per-unit cost?"
- "Are there additional fees?"

### If Sample Quality is Poor
- Be honest but tactful: "The sample doesn't quite match our vision..."
- Ask for revision or explore other suppliers
- Don't force a partnership with a poor supplier

### If Supplier Is Unreliable
- Document the issue
- Move to backup supplier (Tier 2)
- Don't sign long-term commitment
