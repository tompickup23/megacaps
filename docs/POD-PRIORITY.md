## COMMON QUESTIONS

**Q: What's the catch?**  
A: No catch. POD margins are lower than manufacturing, but zero inventory risk + zero work = passive income potential. 100–300 orders/month at £14 profit = £1,400–£4,200/month with <20 hours/month work.

**Q: Why not manufacture + hold inventory?**  
A: You said "zero inventory, hands-off fulfillment." Manufacturing requires MOQ (50+ units), inventory holding, shipping logistics, customer service. POD is truly hands-off.

**Q: Can I scale to thousands of orders?**  
A: Yes. POD scales automatically. Same process works for 100 orders or 10,000 orders. No additional work.

**Q: What if POD quality is bad?**  
A: Test with samples first (covered in POD-OUTREACH.md). Printful embroidery is professional-grade. If unsatisfied, switch providers.

**Q: How long until first sale?**  
A: 2–3 weeks from today if you move fast. Setup time: 5 hours over a week.

**Q: Can I do this part-time?**  
A: Yes. Phase 1 = 10–15 hours/month. Phase 2 = 15–20 hours/month. Could be weekend project.

**Q: What's the hardest part?**  
A: Marketing (getting customers), not fulfillment. POD handles all logistics. You need to drive traffic.
