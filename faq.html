Care & Maintenance

Hand wash only: Use warm water and mild detergent. Gently scrub with a soft-bristle brush. Rinse thoroughly with cool water. Never use a washing machine or tumble dryer — this will damage the embroidery and shape.

Air dry only. Remove excess water by gently squeezing (don't wring). Place on a clean cloth or hat stand to dry naturally. Avoid direct sunlight, heat sources, and the tumble dryer. The cap will retain its shape and colour better this way.

Our 3D embroidery is designed to last. With proper care (hand wash, air dry), your cap will maintain its appearance for years. The metallic gold details on our Navy and Black & Gold editions may develop a natural patina — many customers love this aged look.

Absolutely. While still damp from cleaning, gently curve or shape the brim as you prefer. Our structured cotton twill is forgiving and will hold the shape once dry. This is one of the perks of a quality cap.
