Everything you need to know about MEGA Caps, from delivery and sizing to wholesale.

How long does delivery take?

Standard UK delivery takes 3-5 business days. We ship next business day for orders placed before 2 PM GMT. International orders (EU, US, rest of world) take 7-14 business days depending on destination.

What are your shipping costs?

  • US & rest of world: £8.50 standard, £18.00 express

All prices include Royal Mail or international carrier tracking. Exact delivery cost is confirmed at checkout.

Can I track my order?

Absolutely. You'll receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking link as soon as your order ships.

Payment

Card payments are handled by Stripe on a secure checkout page, so your card details never touch our servers. You can also reserve an order by emailing hello@megacaps.co.uk while payment goes live, and we'll hold your caps and confirm by reply.

Returns & Refunds

What's your returns policy?

We offer a 30-day returns window from the date you receive your order. Caps must be unworn, unaltered, and in original packaging to qualify for a full refund. Please see our full returns policy for details.

How do I initiate a return?

Email us at hello@megacaps.co.uk with your order number and reason. We'll send you a prepaid Royal Mail returns label. Once we receive and verify your return, your refund will process within 5-7 business days.

Do you accept exchanges?

Yes. If you want a different size or colour, we can arrange an exchange for free within 30 days of purchase, provided the original cap is unworn. Contact us at hello@megacaps.co.uk to arrange.

Sizing & Fit

What size cap should I buy?

All our caps feature an adjustable snapback closure, so one size fits most adults (head circumference 54-62 cm). If you're unsure, open the size guide link on any product card for detailed measurements and international size conversions.

Do you have kids' sizes?

Not currently, but kids can often wear our adjustable caps with the snapback pulled tight. If you have a very young child, contact us at hello@megacaps.co.uk. We're planning youth sizes soon.

Can I return a cap if it doesn't fit?

Yes. Fit is covered under our 30-day returns policy. If the cap doesn't fit after adjusting the snapback, it qualifies for a free exchange or refund. Contact us at hello@megacaps.co.uk.

How do I measure my head?

Use a soft measuring tape and wrap it around your head at the widest point, just above your ears. This gives your head circumference in centimetres. Match it against the size guide on any product card to find the right fit.

Care & Maintenance

How should I clean my MEGA cap?

Hand wash only: use warm water and mild detergent. Gently scrub with a soft-bristle brush, then rinse thoroughly with cool water. Never use a washing machine or tumble dryer, as this will damage the embroidery and shape.

How do I dry my cap?

Air dry only. Remove excess water by gently squeezing (don't wring). Place on a clean cloth or hat stand to dry naturally. Avoid direct sunlight, heat sources, and the tumble dryer. The cap will retain its shape and colour better this way.

Will the embroidery fade?

Our 3D embroidery is designed to last. With proper care (hand wash, air dry), your cap will maintain its appearance for years. The metallic gold details on our Navy and Black & Gold editions may develop a natural patina, and many customers love this aged look.

Can I reshape the brim?

Absolutely. While still damp from cleaning, gently curve or shape the brim as you prefer. Our structured cotton twill is forgiving and will hold the shape once dry. This is one of the perks of a quality cap.
