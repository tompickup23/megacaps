Built for the Terraces, the Pub and the Town Square

MEGA started with a simple idea: England deserves a cap worth wearing. Not a flimsy souvenir, but a properly made six-panel cap with real embroidery, a structured crown and colours that do not fade after one summer.

Every design is checked in England before it ships to you, and every order is backed by a 30-day no-quibble return.

Size Guide

How to measure

Wrap a soft measuring tape around your head at the widest point, just above your ears, and read the measurement in centimetres.

Head circumference (cm)UK/EU sizeFit
54 to 56XSSmall

All MEGA caps have an adjustable closure, so one size fits most adults (54 to 62 cm). Tighten or loosen for a comfortable, personal fit.
