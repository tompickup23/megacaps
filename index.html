Size Guide

How to Measure

Use a soft measuring tape and wrap it around your head at the widest point (just above your ears). Record the measurement in centimetres.

Size Conversion Chart

Head Circumference (cm) UK/EU Size US Size Fit

Our Caps

All MEGA Caps feature an adjustable snapback closure, which means one size fits most adults (head circumference 54-62 cm). The snapback can be tightened or loosened for a comfortable, personalised fit.
