About MEGA Caps

MEGA Caps stands for something simple: pride in who we are and where we come from.

Founded in 2026 by two friends who believe England deserves better than the status quo, we create premium quality caps for those who aren't afraid to wear their beliefs on their head.

"Every cap is designed with one goal: to look sharp, feel comfortable, and start conversations."

Whether you're at the pub, the match, or marching for what matters — MEGA has you covered. We refuse to apologize for bold statements. We refuse to blend in. We refuse to be quiet.

What We Believe

  • Bold statements, not half-measures
  • Made for England, worn with pride
  • Dropship and white-label available for partners
