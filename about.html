About MEGA Caps

MEGA Caps was born from a simple idea: premium quality headwear that makes a statement. Our "Make England Great Again" caps are designed in England and built to last.

Whether you're wearing it on the high street or at the match, a MEGA cap says you're proud of where you come from. We use quality materials, bold embroidery, and structured fits that look good on everyone.

We also offer white-label and dropship options for businesses and resellers. Get in touch to discuss bulk orders.
