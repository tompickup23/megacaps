About MEGA Caps

Premium caps designed in England, made to look sharp, feel comfortable and start conversations.

MEGA Caps stands for something simple: pride in who we are and where we come from.

Founded in 2026 by two friends who believe England deserves better than the status quo, we create premium quality caps for people who wear their beliefs on their head. Whether you are at the pub, the match, or marching for what matters, MEGA has you covered. We refuse to apologise for bold statements. We refuse to blend in. We refuse to be quiet.

What We Believe

  • Quality over quantity. Buy once, wear for years.
  • Bold statements, not half-measures.
  • Made for England, worn with pride.
