# MEGA Caps Brand Guide

## Identity

**Name:** MEGA Caps
**Full name:** Make England Great Again
**Concept:** Bold, patriotic English pride with premium execution. Box-logo
recognition, heritage colours, embroidery-first design.

## Logo system (rebuilt 27 Aug 2026)

All marks are SVG with **baked letterform paths** (converted from Archivo
Black, licensed under the SIL Open Font License). They render identically
everywhere; no font needs to be installed. The old `<text>`-based SVGs were
removed because they fell back to whatever font the viewer had.

| File | What it is | Use |
|------|------------|-----|
| `icon.svg` | St George shield crest, gold border | Favicon, app icon, avatar, cap side patch |
| `logo-box.svg` | Red box, white MEGA | Primary box logo, social banners |
| `logo-box-navy.svg` | Navy box, gold MEGA | Premium placements |
| `logo-box-white.svg` | White box, red MEGA, red keyline | Light backgrounds |
| `logo-box-black.svg` | Black box, gold MEGA | Limited edition contexts |
| `logo-primary.svg` | Crest + MEGA + strapline, dark on light | Website, print, press |
| `logo-primary-dark.svg` | Crest + MEGA + strapline, light on dark | Navy/black backgrounds |

Regeneration script (crest geometry + font-to-path conversion) lives with the
build tooling; letterforms come from `ArchivoBlack-Regular.ttf`.

## Typography

- **Display:** Archivo Black (Google Fonts, OFL). All caps, tight leading.
- **Body:** Inter 400/600/700 (Google Fonts, OFL).
- On-cap embroidery uses heavy block capitals matching the wordmark.

## Voice

- Patriotic but not preachy. Pride in England without party politics.
- Confident and direct. Short sentences. British English.
- Premium positioning: materials and construction do the boasting.
- House rule: no em-dashes anywhere, on any surface.

## Photography

Product shots: single cap, three-quarter hero angle, floating on warm
light-grey seamless background, soft diffused light, visible fabric and
thread texture. Lifestyle: candid, faces not shown, terraces/pub/street,
warm cinematic grade. Hero imagery: British heritage (Spitfire, white
cliffs), warm golden hour, navy shadows.
