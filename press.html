Brand Overview

MEGA Caps is a premium British caps brand designed for people who prefer to stand out. We create bold, high-quality caps that celebrate English pride and patriotism. Launched in 2026, MEGA is building a community of customers who value craftsmanship, confidence, and unapologetic British identity.

Tagline: "Make England Great Again. Premium Quality Caps."

Founded: 2026 • Based: England, UK • E-commerce: megacaps.co.uk

Typography

  • Display font: Archivo Black, used for headlines and the wordmark
  • Body font: Inter (400, 600, 700), modern and highly readable
  • Recommended fallbacks: Arial Black for display; -apple-system, Segoe UI, Helvetica, Arial for body

Tone & Voice

  • Confident: command attention without apology
  • Patriotic: celebrate English pride authentically, not aggressively
  • Premium: emphasise quality, craftsmanship, and value
  • Witty: subtle humour, clever wordplay
  • Direct: strong energy, action-oriented copy

Logo & Assets

The primary lockup pairs the MEGA crest with the wordmark in Archivo Black. Use the boxed logo on solid backgrounds and the crest icon alone at small sizes.

Usage Rules

  • Never distort, stretch, or recolour the logo
  • Maintain clear space equal to the height of the letter "M"
  • Use the supplied light and dark variants rather than inverting colours yourself
  • Minimum size: 60px wide for web use

Founder

Greg Rothwell, Founder & Creative Director

Greg is a British entrepreneur and designer with a passion for quality products and unapologetic patriotism. Based in Rossendale, north of Manchester, he founded MEGA Caps in early 2026 to create premium caps for people who value boldness and craftsmanship. Greg combines design expertise with a deep understanding of his community, creating products that resonate with English pride.

"Every cap is designed with one goal: to look sharp, feel comfortable, and start conversations."
Greg Rothwell, Founder

MEGA Caps operates as a lean, focused team. For questions about design, production, or operations, contact hello@megacaps.co.uk.
