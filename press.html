Brand Overview

MEGA Caps is a premium British caps brand designed for those who aren't afraid to stand out. We create bold, high-quality caps that celebrate English pride and patriotism. Launched in 2026, MEGA has rapidly built a community of customers who value craftsmanship, confidence, and unapologetic British identity.

Tagline: "Make England Great Again — Premium Quality Caps"

Founded: 2026 | Based: England, UK | E-commerce: megacaps.co.uk

Typography

  • Primary Font: Inter (sans-serif) — modern, professional, highly readable
  • Display Font: Inter Heavy (900 weight) — bold headlines and branding
  • Recommended Fallback: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto

Tone & Voice

  • Confident: Command attention without apology
  • Patriotic: Celebrate English pride authentically, not aggressively
  • Premium: Emphasise quality, craftsmanship, and value
  • Witty: Subtle humour, clever wordplay
  • Masculine: Strong energy, action-oriented copy

Logo & Assets

Primary Logo: "MEGA" wordmark in 900-weight Inter, set in red (#C8102E) or white (on dark backgrounds).

MEGA

Primary logo — use on white backgrounds

Logo Usage Rules:

  • Never distort or stretch the logo
  • Maintain clear space (1x the height of the letter "M")
  • Use red (#C8102E) or white only — no other colours
  • Minimum size: 60px wide for web use
  • Always use a professional typeface to render the logo
