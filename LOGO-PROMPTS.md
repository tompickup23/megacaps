# MEGA Caps — Logo Prompt Bank

Ready-to-paste prompts for Canva Pro AI (or Midjourney/DALL-E).
Goal: **50 logo variations** for "Make England Great Again" baseball caps.

---

## Design Brief

- **Text:** "Make England Great Again" or "MEGA"
- **Use case:** Embroidered / printed on a structured baseball cap
- **Constraints:** Must be legible at small scale, work on dark and light cap colours, no fine detail that won't embroider well
- **Colour palette:** Red (#C8102E), White (#FFFFFF), Blue (#012169), Gold (#D4A843) — St George's Cross / English flag tones
- **Themes:** St George's Cross, English heritage, bold patriotic, old English typography, royal insignia, lion motifs

---

## Cap Logo Design Principles Applied

1. **Simplicity** — fewer elements = cleaner embroidery and better recognition at distance
2. **Bold type** — thick strokes that hold up when stitched or heat-pressed
3. **Limited colours** — 2-4 max for production cost and visual clarity
4. **Contrast** — light on dark or dark on light, never mid-tone on mid-tone
5. **Symmetry / balance** — centered composition suits the front panel of a cap
6. **Icon + wordmark** — a small emblem above or beside the text gives flexibility (icon-only on side, full lockup on front)
7. **Scalable** — must look good from 2 inches wide (side logo) to 4 inches (front panel)

## How to Use These Prompts

1. **Canva Pro:** Go to Canva > Create a Design > Custom (1000x1000px) > Apps > Text to Image. Paste each prompt.
2. **Midjourney:** Prefix with `/imagine` and append `--ar 1:1 --style raw --v 6.1`
3. **DALL-E / ChatGPT:** Paste directly into the image generation chat.

### Recommended Canva Settings
- **Style:** choose "None" or "Minimalist" for cleanest results
- **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 (square)
- **Generate 4 at a time**, pick the best, then use "Edit image" to refine

### After Generating
- Save favourites to `images/logos/` in this repo
- Name them descriptively: `cross-shield-v1.png`, `gothic-mega-v2.png`, etc.
- Greg and Tom review and shortlist the top 5 in a GitHub Issue or in TASKS.md
